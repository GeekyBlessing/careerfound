# Homepage photography

The homepage compositions pair real people with the real CareerFound interface.
Each slot below is off until its file exists; the layout then switches to a
device-only composition automatically (no broken images). To turn a slot on,
drop the file here and set `available: true` for it in `src/lib/photos.ts`.

Shoot or choose photos of real people actually working, not posed pointing at a
screen. Keep faces in the upper two thirds of the frame so the laptop that
overlaps the lower part of the photo never covers them. Warm, natural light.
No visible brand logos or screens showing a third party's product. Use only
photos you have the rights and consent to publish.

| Slot | File | Subject | Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hero | `hero-learner.jpg` | A learner at a desk, looking at a laptop or notebook, mid-work | 2000 x 1500 |
| discover | `discover-tablet.jpg` | A person answering questions on a tablet, thinking | 2000 x 1500 |
| projectLab | `project-lab-laptop.jpg` | A person at a laptop in a terminal or editor, absorbed (the strongest section) | 2400 x 1500 |
| portfolio | `portfolio-review.jpg` | A person showing finished work to someone off camera, or reviewing it alone | 2000 x 1500 |

Mentor portraits live in `public/mentors/` and are already in use
(`toriola.jpg`, `mobile-engineering-mentor.jpg`). Portrait crops (4 x 5) work
best; each added mentor needs a real, verified person and their written consent.

Export JPEG at quality 80 or WebP, under 400 KB each.
